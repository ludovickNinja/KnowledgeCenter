## Brand families

We sell under three brand families. Classic covers plain and traditional bands. Signature covers designer and diamond-set styles. Forge covers alternative-metal men's bands.

## Autumn 2026 collection

The Autumn 2026 collection adds 12 styles to the Signature family, including the first two-tone tantalum styles. Retailers receive the lookbook and pricing on October 1. Orders open October 15.

## Discontinued styles

Four Classic styles were retired on October 1, 2026. Existing orders are honoured. New orders for retired styles must be quoted as custom work.
