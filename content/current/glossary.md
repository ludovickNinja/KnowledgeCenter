## Order terms

PO: purchase order from a retailer. WO: work order, the internal job created from a PO. ETA: promised ship date, not delivery date. RMA: return merchandise authorization, required before any item is sent back.

## Product terms

Finger size: ring size in the North American scale. Width: band width in millimetres. Fit: the interior profile of a band, either standard or comfort fit. Finish: the surface treatment, such as polished, satin or hammered.

## Production terms

Casting: pouring molten metal into a mould. Sprue: the channel through which metal enters the mould, removed after casting. Bench: a jeweller's workstation. QC: quality control inspection before shipping.
