## Process steps

Wax or resin models are attached to a sprue tree, invested in plaster, burned out in the kiln, then cast under vacuum. Trees are quenched, divested and cut before going to the bench.

## Alloy control

Each alloy has its own crucible. Never mix gold karats or colours in one crucible. Record the alloy lot number on the casting sheet for every tree.

## Common defects

Porosity is usually from an overheated flask or a wet investment. Incomplete fill is usually from a cold flask or a thin sprue. Log every defect on the casting sheet so patterns are visible.
