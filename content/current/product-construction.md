## Cast vs die-struck

Cast rings are made by pouring metal into a mould and are best for detailed designs. Die-struck rings are stamped from solid metal under high pressure, producing a denser, more durable band. Most of our plain bands are die-struck.

## Fit profiles

Standard fit is flat on the inside. Comfort fit is domed on the inside so the ring slides over the knuckle more easily. Comfort fit bands run about a quarter size larger, so customers used to standard fit should be sized down.

## Finishes

Polished, satin, brushed, hammered and sandblast finishes are available on most bands. Finishes can be refreshed under the care program.
