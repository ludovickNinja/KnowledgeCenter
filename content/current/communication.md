## Channels

Pick the channel by who needs to see it:

- **Teams chat** for quick internal questions
- **Email** for anything a retailer or external party needs to see
- **Ticketing system** for anything that needs tracking

Do not use personal messaging apps for work.

## Response times

Acknowledge retailer emails within 4 business hours even if you do not have the answer yet. Internal Teams messages should be answered the same day.

## Meetings

Every recurring meeting has an owner and an agenda posted the day before. If you cannot attend, send your update in writing to the owner.
