## Where we started

The company was founded in 2001 as a two-person workshop making custom wedding bands for local jewellers. The first commercial line launched in 2003 and was carried by a dozen independent retailers within a year.

## Growth milestones

By 2010 we were manufacturing for retailers across Canada and the United States. The current production facility opened in 2014. In 2025 we expanded the Montréal facility to add a second casting line and an in-house finishing department.

## What we make today

We design and manufacture wedding bands, engagement rings and fashion jewellery in precious metals and alternative metals, sold through independent jewellers and select retail chains under several brand names.
