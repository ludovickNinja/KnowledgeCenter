## Personal protective equipment

Safety glasses are required at every bench and in the casting area. Respirators are required at the polishing lathe. Tie back long hair and remove loose jewellery before operating any rotary tool.

## Torches and gas

Check hoses for cracks at the start of every shift. Close the gas valve at the tank, not just the torch, at the end of the shift.

## Chemicals

Pickle solution is acidic. Use copper tongs only; steel tongs will plate your work. Report any spill larger than a cup to the Production Lead immediately.
