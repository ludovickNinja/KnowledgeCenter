## RMA required

No return, repair or resize is accepted without an RMA number issued by Customer Service. Items received without an RMA are held for 10 days and then returned to the retailer at their cost.

## Return window

Unworn stock items may be returned within 30 days of invoice for full credit. Returns between 31 and 60 days are subject to a 15% restocking fee. Custom and engraved items are not returnable.

## Resizing

Precious-metal bands can be resized up to two sizes in either direction at no charge within the first year. Tungsten, cobalt, titanium and tantalum bands cannot be resized; offer an exchange under the care program instead.

## Lifetime care program

The care program covers refinishing, rhodium plating and prong tightening for the life of the piece. Shipping to us is paid by the retailer; return shipping is paid by us.
