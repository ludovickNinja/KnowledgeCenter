## Precious metals

Gold is sold by karat: 10K is 41.7% gold, 14K is 58.3%, 18K is 75%. White gold is alloyed with palladium or nickel and usually rhodium plated. Platinum is 95% pure and heavier than gold.

## Alternative metals

Tungsten carbide, titanium, cobalt and tantalum are used for men's bands. They are hard, lightweight or both, but most cannot be resized. Always confirm finger size before production on alternative metals.

## Stones

We set natural diamonds, lab-grown diamonds and selected coloured gemstones. Every stone over 0.30 carat ships with a grading report. Lab-grown and natural stones must never be mixed in the same piece without documentation.
