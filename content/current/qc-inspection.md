## Dimensions

Finger size must be within a quarter size of the work order. Width must be within ±0.10 mm of the specified width. Measure at three points around the band.

## Stamps and marks

Every piece carries the metal quality stamp and the maker's mark. Check that the stamp is legible and matches the work order metal. Pieces with the wrong stamp are scrapped, never corrected.

## Stone security

Run a fingernail over every prong and run the tap test: tap the ring on a wooden block and listen for a rattle. Any movement fails inspection.

## Finish

Inspect under 10x magnification for scratches, pits and polishing compound in recesses. Compare the finish to the reference sample for that style.
