## Departments

There are four operating departments: Sales & Customer Service, Production & Quality, Marketing, and Operations & Shipping. Finance, People & Culture and Learning & Development are shared services.

## Who to ask

Order and pricing questions go to Customer Service. Production timelines go to the Production Lead. Shipping and carrier issues go to Operations. Documentation and training questions go to Learning & Development.

## Escalation

Escalate to your department lead first. Cross-department issues are escalated by the lead to the weekly operations meeting.
