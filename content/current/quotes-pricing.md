## Quote validity

Quotes on precious-metal items are valid for 7 calendar days because they are tied to the daily metal price. Quotes on alternative-metal items are valid for 30 days.

## Discounts

Tier pricing is applied automatically by the ERP. Any discount beyond tier pricing requires written approval from Sales Leadership before the quote is sent.

## Custom work

Custom quotes require a CAD rendering and a Production review of feasibility before pricing. Allow 3 business days.
