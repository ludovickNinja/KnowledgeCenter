## How we sell

We are a wholesale manufacturer. We do not sell to consumers. Our customers are independent jewellers and retail chains, who sell our products under our brand names or their own.

## Retailer tiers

Retailers are grouped into three tiers based on annual volume. Tier affects pricing, minimum order quantities and marketing support. Tier assignments are reviewed every January by Sales Leadership.

## What retailers expect

Accurate ETAs, consistent quality and fast answers. A late order with warning is forgivable. A late order with no warning costs us the account.
