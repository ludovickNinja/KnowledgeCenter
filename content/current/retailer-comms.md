## Tone

Professional, warm and specific. Lead with the answer, then the detail. Never blame another department in writing to a retailer.

## Delays

If an ETA will be missed, tell the retailer before the ETA passes, give the new date, and say what caused the delay in one sentence.

## Templates

Use the approved templates in the shared mailbox for order confirmations, ETA updates and RMA instructions. Edit the details, not the structure.
