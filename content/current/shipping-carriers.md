## Insurance

Every shipment is insured for full invoice value. Shipments over $10,000 require signature on delivery and must be split across two boxes.

## Tracking

The tracking number is entered in the ERP on the sales order the same day the box leaves. The ERP sends the retailer an automatic notification.
