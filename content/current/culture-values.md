## Craft first

Every piece we ship carries someone's name on the most important day of their life. We take the time to do it right, and we say so when something is not right.

## Retailer partnership

Our retailers are partners, not accounts. We win when they win. Honest communication about timelines and problems is expected from every department.

## Continuous improvement

Anyone can propose a process change. Proposals go through the department lead and are reviewed in the monthly improvement meeting.
