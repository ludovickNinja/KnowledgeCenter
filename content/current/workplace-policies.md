## Hours and attendance

Standard hours are 8:00 to 16:30 with a 30-minute unpaid lunch. Shift start times vary by department. Report absences to your supervisor before the start of your shift.

## Secure areas and badge access

The vault, casting area and finishing room are badge-restricted. Never hold a door for someone without a badge. Lost badges must be reported to Operations the same day so they can be deactivated.

## Breaks

Two paid 15-minute breaks per full shift, taken at times set by the department schedule. Production breaks are staggered so that casting is never unattended.

## Time off

Vacation requests are submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Requests are approved by your supervisor in the order received.
