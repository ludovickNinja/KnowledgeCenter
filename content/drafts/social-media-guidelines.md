## Brand voice

Confident, warm and specific. We talk about craft and the people who wear our pieces, not about ourselves. Avoid superlatives we cannot back up.

## Approvals

Any post that names a retailer, shows pricing or mentions an unreleased collection needs written approval from the Marketing lead before it is scheduled.

## What we never post

- Customer or retailer information without consent
- Production photos that show badge-restricted areas
- Pricing comparisons with other manufacturers
